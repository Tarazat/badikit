## Help & Details

### 1. Dates

The Bahá’í Calendar is known as the Badí‘ Calendar.

The current date is displayed at the top of the Kit screen. Each day begins at sunset.

More details can be found at Bahai.org https://www.bahai.org/action/devotional-life/calendar

### 2. Qiblih Compass

The compass can be used as a reference tool to find the direction towards the Shrine of Bahá’u’lláh.

Bearing is calculated relative to 0° true north (not magenetic north). The shortest path over the earth's surface can also be viewed by swiping on the compass panel.

#### How to use

Align the red and blue compass hands.
* **1.** Hold your device parallel to the ground, directly in front of you and pointing outward
* **2.** Tap inside the compass circle to activate the tracking
* **3.** Rotate your body slowly until you feel or see a confirmation
